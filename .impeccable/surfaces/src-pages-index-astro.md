## Scope and mode
Full-blog visual redesign covering the chronological home indexes, article pages, tag indexes and filters, and 404. Visitor mode: Read.

## Audience and job
Chinese- and English-language readers need to scan the archive by year/date, open an article, and read long-form content without interface noise. Secondary tasks are tag browsing, locale switching, theme switching, and RSS discovery.

## Content and constraints
Use only the existing posts, dates, tags, reading metadata, and localized copy. Preserve current routes and static GitHub Pages behavior. The reference `https://jeiwan.net/` is binding for the narrow editorial column, serif-led voice, chronological rhythm, underlined links, and minimal ornament; adapt rather than remove existing bilingual and dark-theme functions.

## Chosen direction
A restrained personal web journal: paper-like light field, ink-like dark field, compact masthead, year-led archive ledger, and quiet text controls. The first viewport opens directly on identity and the newest archive year rather than a card grid or promotional hero. Signature interaction: archive-link underlines draw across like a ledger rule while the adjacent date gains contrast, with an immediate reduced-motion fallback.
